# 05: The person approves the Contract, and it is versioned

**What to build:** The Contract is the Plan Mode of aidea for a whole project: the person approves it, as one approves a plan in an agent's plan mode, and from then it is the plan the Builders work from. An approval has a version and a date; a change after approval is a Proposal the person accepts and makes a new version. Until it is approved the Contract is a draft.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately).

**Status:** needs-info: the person decided on 2026-11-05 that the Contract is only a summary computed from the accepted layers, for now. A draft, an approval and versions need something stored. Decide first what is stored (for example a recorded approval of a snapshot of the layers) or whether the Contract becomes an object of its own.

- [ ] The Contract has draft and approved states; Approve is the person's action only.
- [ ] An approved Contract keeps its versions and shows what changed between them.
- [ ] A change after approval needs an accepted Proposal (tested through the protocol).
- [ ] The state and version show in the section and in the legend, in words as well as by look.
